# AgentOS pricing page pattern

## Context

Use this reference when building or briefing a pricing page for AgentOS by PerformOS, the private AI team offer for business leaders.

AgentOS pricing currently has two tiers:

- Standard: $4,999 AUD/month + $3,000 AUD setup
- Advanced: $4,999 AUD/month + $6,000 AUD setup
- Both: up to 10 custom AI agents
- Both: six-month minimum engagement

The tier difference is reasoning depth, not agent count.

## Core positioning

Lead with business outcomes, not model sizes.

Preferred headline:

**Private AI team for business leaders**

Preferred subheading:

**AgentOS gives your business up to 10 custom AI agents, built around your workflows, governed by your permissions, and managed by PerformOS. Choose the model tier that matches the level of reasoning your leaders need.**

## Tier framing

### Standard

Plain-English positioning:

**Fast, practical AI agents for everyday business work.**

Use for:

- meeting preparation
- drafting and rewriting
- document summaries
- internal FAQs
- leadership support
- follow-up tracking
- basic analysis
- standard reporting
- workflow guidance

Technical explanation only if needed:

Uses 8 billion parameter models, tuned for speed, privacy, and reliable business support.

### Advanced

Plain-English positioning:

**Higher-capability AI agents for complex business reasoning.**

Use for:

- board and executive preparation
- strategy review
- complex decision support
- multi-document synthesis
- risk analysis
- legal-style reasoning support
- product and market analysis
- complex operating problems
- advanced reporting and dashboard logic

Technical explanation only if needed:

Uses larger 14 to 70 billion parameter models, selected for deeper reasoning, stronger analysis, and more nuanced judgement.

Avoid making Standard look weak with too many blunt crosses.

Prefer comparison labels like:

- Included

## Setup fee explanation

Use this logic:

AgentOS is not a generic AI subscription. Each private AI team is configured around the client’s business, source documents, workflows, permissions, leadership needs, and approved tools.

The setup fee covers discovery, agent design, configuration, testing, governance setup, and go-live support.

Standard setup is lighter because the agents use everyday business models and simpler task patterns.

Advanced setup is deeper because the agents require stronger reasoning models, more careful configuration, and more testing against complex leadership use cases.

## FAQ points

Include these:

- Do both tiers include 10 agents? Yes.
- What is the difference? Reasoning capability.
- What does 8 billion parameters mean? Smaller, faster, everyday business support.
- What does 14 to 70 billion parameters mean? More reasoning capacity for ambiguity, strategy, risk, and deeper synthesis.
- Should buyers understand model sizes? No. Use plain-English tier labels first.
- Can clients upgrade later? Yes.
- Is data kept private? AgentOS is designed around private or local data retention, client-approved sources, permission rules, and human approval controls.
