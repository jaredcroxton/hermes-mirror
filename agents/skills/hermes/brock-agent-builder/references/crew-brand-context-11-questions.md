# CREW Brand-Context — The 11 Practical Questions

These are the canonical 11 questions for `crew-core-brand-context`. They capture who a business is without asking about design (colours, fonts, visual style). Design discovery belongs in the design skills (packs 10, 12, 13, 14), not in brand-context onboarding.

## The questions

1. **What does your business do, and why does it matter?** Two sentences. What you make, sell, or provide. And why anyone should care.

2. **Who buys from you? Start with the person who pays. Then tell me about anyone else who matters.** Describe your primary customer. Then: anyone else who uses or influences the decision? Different age, different need, different problem.

3. **Why would a customer leave you?** The real reason. What made someone walk away in the past? What do customers consistently misunderstand?

4. **If your business was a person at a dinner party, how would they show up?** What would they wear? How would they speak? Would they arrive early to help set up, slide in late and apologetic, or exactly on time with a bottle of wine? Warm host? Quiet expert? Loud storyteller? This is your voice.

5. **What's the one thing you always get right?** What do customers compliment? What do they tell their friends?

6. **What are you trying to achieve right now?** What does success look like in six months?

7. **What's your website? And where else do people find you online?** URL. Social accounts. Review pages. Anywhere a customer might comment or ask a question.

8. **What's something unwritten that a new person would need to know?** The unwritten rules. The way things actually get done. What would surprise an outsider.

9. **Where do you feel you let your customers down?** The gap between what you wish you could deliver and what you can reliably deliver today.

10. **Is there anything I must know to get this right?** Regulations. Sensitive topics. History that shapes decisions. Anything that would embarrass you if I got it wrong.

11. **What question haven't I asked that I should have?** The safety net.

## Design split

These questions deliberately exclude design vocabulary (colours, fonts, visual style, imagery). The design packs (10, 12, 13, 14) carry their own 7-question discovery pre-build flow that handles look-and-feel. A business owner should never have to think about hex codes during onboarding.

The original 12-question set included design questions that were removed because:
- A florist, accountant, or cafe owner cannot meaningfully answer "what visual style feels right"
- Design vocabulary leaked into business onboarding, confusing non-designers
- The design skills already scrape the website and run their own discovery

## Output

The answers produce `brand-context.md` at `.claude/crew-state/brand-context.md`. Every CREW skill reads this file on startup (Step 0).
