# Eventbrite Workshop Listing — Copy Pattern

Captured 29 June 2026 from the CREW one-day workshop listing build.

## Key principle

Write from the buyer's perspective. Use language they understand. Strip ALL internal product terminology. Jared's correction: "don't worry about gold standard, because that doesn't make any sense to the person."

## What to strip

Never use these in public-facing event copy:
- "gold standard" — means nothing to a buyer
- "QA pass" — internal quality metric
- Product names (Vercel, Supabase, Firecrawl) — use "the internet," "a live database," "a scraping tool"
- "94 agents" without context — say "92 AI agents installed into your Claude Code"
- Internal pack names (Bedrock, Fuel, Engine)
- "Deploy to Vercel" — say "deploy to the internet" or "go live"

## What buyers understand

- "Full stack apps that connect to databases"
- "Live on the web"
- "Working output, not notes"
- "You built yourself"
- "Real tools"

## Eventbrite field formats

### Summary (140 char max)
One line. What, where, format. Never say "online" if it's in-person.

### Description
Four beats:
1. What it is (one paragraph)
2. Who it's for / prerequisites (one paragraph)
3. What you walk out with (bullet list)
4. Price, location, seats (one line)

### Agenda
Time-block format. Three phases: I Do, We Do, You Do.
Exact times. No em dashes.
Keep setup steps generic (don't name specific services).

### What to bring
Minimal. Laptop, charger, Claude subscription.
Do NOT list GitHub, Docker, Node.js in public copy — put those in the setup guide sent after booking.

### FAQ
Five questions max. Practical, not marketing.
1. Do I need coding experience?
2. What do I need to bring?
3. What do I leave with?
4. What if I fall behind?
5. What is the refund policy?

## Jared's specific corrections

- "We don't say Vercel. We say deploy to the internet."
- "We don't say Supabase. We say connect to a live database."
- "We don't say gold standard. The buyer doesn't know what that means."
- "Don't list GitHub, Docker, Node. Just laptop, charger, Claude subscription. We send the full setup guide after booking."
- "Claude Max at $167 for the one month — frame it as a recommendation because we build all day."
