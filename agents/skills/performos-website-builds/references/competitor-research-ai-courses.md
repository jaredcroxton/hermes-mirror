# Competitor Research — AI Course Homepages (27 May 2026)

Full competitor analysis used to inform the PerformOS 12-week AI course page rebuild. Reference when building education/course landing pages.

## Methodology

Applied the Outlier Research Engine methodology: scrape the actual top performers in the niche, extract section structure, build frequency tables, identify gaps.

## Three key findings

### 1. Nobody shows pricing prominently

Only 1 of 8 sites displayed pricing. Every platform hides it behind "Start Free" CTAs. Implication: $499 upfront is differentiation and a trust signal.

### 2. Instructor credentials are underutilised

Only DeepLearning.AI (celebrity) and Udacity give instructor presentation real estate. For independent providers, Microsoft AI-900/AI-102 certs are a differentiator most marketplaces cannot claim.

### 3. "Personalised" is vacant positioning

No site sells personalised learning as its core differentiator. "Your personal AI tutor" is a blue-ocean claim.

## Recommended beat structure (derived from data)

1. Nav
2. Hero with price in eyebrow (differentiation from finding 1)
3. Trust ribbon (substitute for partner logos — finding 2)
4. Problem contrast ("Most AI courses treat you like everyone else")
5. How you learn (differentiation from finding 3)
6. How it works (essential for unfamiliar value prop)
7. Instructor with certs (finding 2 applied)
8. Curriculum overview (universal, 7/8)
9. Certificate of Completion (tangible outcome)
10. Spectrum (audience fit)
11. Pricing (finding 1 applied — transparency as differentiation)
12. Mandatory disclosure (Option B legal requirement)
13. Comparison table
14. FAQ with objection handlers
15. Footer with trademark attribution
