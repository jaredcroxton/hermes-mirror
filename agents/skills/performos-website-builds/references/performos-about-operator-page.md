# PerformOS About page: operator credibility brief pattern

Use when Jared asks for an About page, founder bio, LinkedIn-to-site conversion copy, or a Claude Code brief for an About page on PerformOS.

## Core judgement

The About page is a trust page, not a CV. Its job is to answer:

**Why should a client trust Jared to help their business adopt AI properly?**

Position Jared as an operator, not a theorist. He has led real teams, real markets, real membership systems, and real performance work. Avoid making the page sound like a job application or generic founder bio.

## Public positioning rules

Use:
- Jared Croxton
- Director of Operations and Learning & Development
- Seven APAC markets
- Membership and subscription loyalty environment
- Operations, learning, performance, AI adoption, customer behaviour, team capability
- Operator judgement
- Practical AI systems built around real workflows

Avoid:
- Accor Plus
- telesales
- call centre
- outbound sales
- Core Plus
- side hustle
- hobby project
- guru
- generic “AI expert” claims

## Preferred headline

**Built by an operator, not a theorist.**

## Recommended structure

1. Hero: operator positioning and seven-market scope
2. Why PerformOS exists
3. Operating experience across markets
4. Three proof cards: Operations lens, Learning and capability, AI implementation
5. Credential strip
6. Operating judgement section
7. How this shapes PerformOS
8. CTA

## Credential handling

Use concise credential cards rather than a long biography block. Known credentials/context from this session:

- Google AI Professional Certificate, completed 23 May 2026 via Coursera and Google. Covered AI fundamentals, brainstorming and planning, research and insights, writing and communication, content creation, data analysis, and app building.
- Microsoft Learn: Designing Agent Architecture and SDLC Integration, completed 20 May 2026.
- Anthropic: AI Fluency for Educators.
- Master of Business Psychology through Edith Cowan University.
- MIT Agentic AI for Organisational Transformation modules.

## LinkedIn scrape limitation pattern

LinkedIn may block public scraping behind an auth wall. If blocked, do not stall. Use:

1. Public search snippets.
2. Jared-provided positioning.
3. Existing memory/context.
4. Certificates or screenshots Jared provides.

State plainly in the brief that LinkedIn was blocked if the source set matters.

## Density rules

- No section over 120 words.
- No paragraph over 55 words.
- No CV dump.
- No over-explaining.
- Use cards for proof and credentials.
- Match the new PerformOS sparse visual direction.
