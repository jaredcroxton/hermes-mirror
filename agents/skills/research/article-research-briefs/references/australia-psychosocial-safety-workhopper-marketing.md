# Australia psychosocial safety and Workhopper marketing case bank

Use when Jared asks for deep research into psychological safety, psychosocial hazards, workplace mental health claims, or site-safety marketing for Workhopper/Tortoise & Hare.

## Core positioning

- Best argument: **psychosocial safety is now site safety**.
- For marketing, bridge the terms:
  - **Psychological safety** = workers feel safe to speak up, report hazards, challenge unsafe decisions, admit mistakes, and ask for help.
  - **Psychosocial hazards** = the regulated WHS risk category: job demands, low control, poor support, role clarity, change management, bullying, harassment, violence, traumatic events, poor physical environment, and poor workplace relationships.
- Workhopper positioning: **a site safety listening layer for psychosocial hazards**.
- Sharp line: **The next safety incident may start as silence.**

## Most useful recent Australian evidence, newest first

1. **22 Sep 2026 — SafeWork NSW Psychosocial Advisory Service**
   - Psychosocial requests for service more than doubled over five years.
   - 1 Jul 2025 to 30 Jun 2026: 3,145 psychosocial related requests, 20.5% of all SafeWork NSW RFS.
   - Largest SafeWork NSW psychosocial compliance blitz: 200+ workplaces visited, 80+ improvement notices for psychosocial hazards/risk management.
   - Source: https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/news/safework-media-releases/minns-labor-government-launches-psychosocial-advisory-service-to-address-the-rise-in-psychosocial-incidents-in-the-workplace

2. **26 Aug 2026 — SafeWork NSW psychological health and safety guidance**
   - Psychological injury claims rose from 7,289 in 2021-22 to 13,648 in 2024-25, an 87% rise. Non-psychological claims rose 9% in the same period.
   - Source: https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/resource-library/psychosocial/psychological-health-and-safety

3. **7 Aug 2026 — WorkSafe Victoria psychological health trials**
   - $6.9m in trials to reduce psychological injuries.
   - In 2025, WorkSafe accepted 2,400+ psychological injury claims, including 682 in health care/social assistance, 644 in public administration/safety, and 322 in education/training.
   - Source: https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/news/2026-08/new-trials-reduce-psychological-injuries-work

4. **1 Jul 2026 — SafeWork NSW 2026-27 regulatory priorities**
   - Psychosocial risks named as one of four key priorities alongside falls, hazardous substances, and mobile plant/vehicles/fixed machinery.
   - Prior 12 months: 2,200+ psychosocial requests for service and 190+ workplace incidents related to psychosocial hazards.
   - Source: https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/news/safework-media-releases/safework-nsw-redefines-its-priorities-in-new-2026-27-regulatory-statement

5. **19 Dec 2025 — Department of Defence conviction**
   - Defence convicted and fined $188,000 for failing to manage psychosocial risks relating to the death of a RAAF worker.
   - Comcare called it the first Commonwealth employer conviction for failing to manage psychosocial risks under federal WHS laws.
   - Source: https://www.comcare.gov.au/about/news-events/news/defence-convicted-after-raaf-workers-death

6. **23 Sep 2025 — WorkSafe WA discontinued Department of Justice prosecution**
   - Prosecution about psychological injury to a former Bunbury Regional Prison officer discontinued after fresh evidence.
   - Not a conviction. Use only as evidence that regulators are willing to investigate/prosecute serious psychosocial harm allegations.
   - Source: https://www.worksafe.wa.gov.au/announcements/worksafe-discontinues-department-of-justice-prosecution-relation-bunbury-regional-prison

7. **2 Sep 2025 — UTS redundancy pause**
   - SafeWork NSW issued a prohibition notice pausing UTS redundancy consultation because of risk of psychological harm. Lifted after UTS extended timelines and changed communications.
   - Source: https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/nsw-safety-regulator-psychosocial-safety

8. **6 Aug 2025 — George v State of NSW [2025] NSWDC 292**
   - Former Detective Sergeant Ralph George awarded $953,244 for psychiatric injury caused by workplace bullying and harassment.
   - Use as a damages case: bullying is not culture noise; it can become a major liability event.
   - Source overview: https://www.hrleader.com.au/law/27182-state-to-pay-just-shy-of-1m-to-bullied-cop

9. **1 Aug 2025 — Magar v Khan [2025] FCA 874**
   - Federal Court awarded $305,000 to a former Mad Mex worker for sexual harassment and victimisation.
   - Strong for high-risk worker groups: young, migrant, casual, visa-dependent, low power.
   - Source: https://www.dentons.com/en/insights/articles/2025/september/3/new-damages-benchmark-to-deter-workplace-sexual-harassment

10. **11 Jun 2025 — NSW Police psychological injury costs**
   - ABC reported NSW Police spent about $1.75b on psychological injury compensation over five years to mid-2024. Psychological injury claims made up 74% of compensation costs.
   - Source: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-06-11/nsw-police-auditor-general-report-psychological-injury-claims/105403582

11. **9 May 2025 — SafeWork NSW Mental Health in Construction report**
   - From 2019 to 2024 there were 800+ major workers compensation mental stress claims in construction; harassment and bullying were the most common reason.
   - Inspectors visited 426 sites. 272 notices issued. 24 improvement notices related to psychosocial risks.
   - 77% of surveyed PCBUs/site supervisors said poor mental health is an issue in construction.
   - Source: https://www.safework.nsw.gov.au/about-us/evaluation/findings-report-mental-health-in-construction-2024

12. **31 Mar 2025 — WSLHD prosecution withdrawn**
   - SafeWork NSW withdrew psychosocial risk prosecution after three weeks of hearing and 20 witnesses.
   - Use carefully: complaint/grievance processes can create psychosocial risk, but stress alone is not automatically a WHS breach.
   - Source: https://www.ashurst.com/en/insights/safework-nsw-withdraws-landmark-psychosocial-risk-prosecution/

13. **18 Mar 2025 — NSW Government workers compensation statement**
   - Psychological injury claims doubled in six years.
   - Psychological claims are 12% of total workers compensation claims but 38% of total cost.
   - Average claim cost rose from $146,000 in 2019-20 to $288,542 in 2024-25.
   - Source: https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/workers-compensation-ministerial-statement

14. **11 Dec 2024 — Elisha v Vision Australia [2024] HCA 50**
   - High Court restored around $1.44m in damages linked to psychiatric injury after a flawed termination process.
   - Not WHS, but strong leadership-process risk evidence.
   - Source: https://www.hcourt.gov.au/cases-and-judgments/cases/decided/case-m222024

15. **19 Oct 2023 — Court Services Victoria conviction**
   - CSV convicted and fined $379,157 over toxic workplace culture at the Coroners Court of Victoria.
   - WorkSafe said risks included traumatic material, role conflict, high workloads, poor workplace relationships, bullying, intimidation, verbal abuse, and inappropriate behaviours.
   - Source: https://www.worksafe.vic.gov.au/news/2023-10/court-body-fined-almost-380000-deadly-work-culture

## Best marketing order

For Workhopper decks/pages, lead with:

1. SafeWork NSW 2026 advisory service and 80+ notices.
2. SafeWork NSW 87% rise in psychological injury claims.
3. SafeWork NSW construction report: 800+ major mental stress claims.
4. Defence conviction: $188,000 fine.
5. Court Services Victoria: $379,157 fine.
6. UTS restructure paused by SafeWork NSW.
7. George v NSW: $953,244 bullying damages.
8. Magar v Khan: $305,000 harassment damages.

Narrative: **The claims are rising. Regulators are acting. Construction is exposed. Silence is now a safety risk.**

## Claims to avoid

Avoid: prevents psychological injury, stops claims, guarantees compliance, detects mental illness, diagnoses workers, protects from prosecution, solves workplace mental health.

Use: helps identify psychosocial hazards, surfaces early warning signals, supports consultation and monitoring, helps leaders act earlier, creates anonymised site-level visibility, complements WHS systems, supports safer reporting.
