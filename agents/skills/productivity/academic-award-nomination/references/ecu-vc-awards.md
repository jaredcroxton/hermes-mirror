## ECU Vice-Chancellor's Student Awards

### Category evolution

The 2024 and 2025 awards only had three categories: Contribution to University Life, Community Engagement, and Reconciliation. Career Ready Employability, Outstanding Research, and Research Impact were introduced in 2026. This means Career Ready Employability has no previous winners to benchmark against. The nomination is setting the template for future cohorts.



## Award details

- **Opens:** 13 July 2026
- **Closes:** 28 August 2026 (11:59 pm)
- **Prize:** $5,000 + trophy + certificate per category
- **Eligibility:** Current ECU students enrolled in at least one unit, good academic standing
- **Self-nominations:** Welcome

## Six categories

| Category | HDR only? | What it rewards |
|---|---|---|
| Contribution to University Life | No | Enhancing student experience through leadership and service within ECU |
| Community Engagement | No | Engagement with external communities aligned with ECU's strategic goals |
| Reconciliation | No | Contribution to valuing Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and knowledge |
| Outstanding Research | **Yes** | Research achievement through originality, rigour, innovation, creativity |
| Research Impact | **Yes** | Research making measurable contribution beyond academia |
| ECU Career Ready Employability | No | Commitment to career through career development initiatives and student leadership |

## Evaluation lenses (all categories)

1. Depth of Impact
2. Breadth of Impact
3. Sustainability of Impact
4. Relevance of Impact

Note: For students in paid roles, assessment is based on contribution *beyond reasonably expected paid work.*

## Nomination form structure (all categories)

Every form has:
- Nominee details (name, student ID)
- Nominator details (name, phone, position, organisation)
- Endorsement details (ECU Dean of School or Director — mandatory)
- **Summary** section (500 words max): initiatives, role, and how contribution goes beyond expected participation
- **Evidence of Impact** section (200 words max): who benefited, measurable outcomes
- **Sustainable Change** section (200 words max): maintainable processes, funding, knowledge sharing, enabling growth
- Up to 4 A4 pages of additional supporting documentation allowed

## Career Ready Employability — specific framing

This category asks for "strong commitment to their future career through active participation in career development initiatives and student leadership."

The strongest narrative for a working professional: map completed coursework subjects directly to professional outcomes at scale, culminating in a tangible career result (promotion, expanded remit, measurable KPI improvement). Include evidence beyond the degree (external courses, side ventures, workshops delivered, products built) to demonstrate that the nominee went beyond what any curriculum asked for.

## Endorsement requirement

Must be endorsed by a relevant ECU Dean of School or Director. External nominators must send the form to the student nominee to obtain endorsement. This is a logistical gate — identify the endorsement pathway early.

## Submission

Via Microsoft Forms link on the awards webpage. Form must be complete with endorsement before deadline.
