# Knowledge base template

Fill this in for the business, then attach it to the assistant (as knowledge) or paste it into the system prompt for a small business. This is what makes the answers A-class, not just the voice. Thin knowledge base equals robotic, wrong answers.

## What we DO NOT quote over the phone

- Fixed prices for unseen work. Only ranges and the callout fee.
- Anything not in the services list. Take a message.

## Emergency triggers (escalate immediately)

- Smell of gas, burst pipe, flooding, no hot water for an elderly person in winter.
- Action: read the owner mobile aloud (<< +61 4xx xxx xxx >>) and tell them to call now.
