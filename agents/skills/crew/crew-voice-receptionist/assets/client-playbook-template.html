The problem

Every unanswered call is a customer ringing your competitor next.

When you are on the tools, driving, or with a customer, the phone rings out. That caller does not leave a voicemail. They scroll to the next business and book with them. For a trades or services business, one missed call a day can be a five-figure hole in the year.

Voicemail is dead

Most callers hang up on voicemail. The ones who leave a message wait hours for a call back, by then they've booked elsewhere.

After-hours is lost revenue

Calls at 7pm or on Sunday go nowhere. That is exactly when people book home services.

A receptionist is $60k+

A human on reception is a salary, super, sick days, and they still can't answer at 2am.

Build it yourself · no code

Seven steps, one afternoon.

Every step is clicking and pasting inside a dashboard. No servers, no code.

  1. Rent an Australian number in Twilio

    Sign in to Twilio, go to Phone Numbers, Buy a Number, set country to Australia, and buy one local number (about $2.50/mo). Then copy your Account SID and Auth Token from the Twilio dashboard and keep them handy.

  2. Create the agent in ElevenLabs

    In ElevenLabs, open Agents and create a new agent. Pick an Australian voice from the library, paste in the system prompt from the kit, and set the first message to your greeting (the one that opens with the AI and recording line).

  3. Feed it your business

    In the agent's Knowledge Base, upload your filled-in knowledge base template: hours, services, what it may quote, your FAQs, and the emergency triggers. This is what makes answers accurate rather than generic.

  4. Connect your number

    In ElevenLabs, open the Phone Numbers tab and add your Twilio number: paste a label, the number, your Twilio SID and Twilio Token, then choose this agent to handle incoming calls. Test by ringing the number.

  5. Add booking and text

    In the agent, click Add Tool, Webhook, and add two tools pointed at your cal.com (check availability, create booking) plus one Twilio tool to text a confirmation. The schemas are in the kit: paste, save, done.

  6. Turn on after-call delivery

    In the agent settings, enable the Post-call webhook and point it at a free Make or Zapier scenario that emails you the transcript and logs the call. Fifteen minutes, once.

  7. Go live with one code on your phone

    On your handset, dial **61*<AI-number>**15# to forward unanswered calls to the agent after 15 seconds. You are live. Watch calls land under Calls History in ElevenLabs. Cancel anytime with ##002#.

Divert codes (Telstra / Optus / Vodafone): No answer **61*<num>**15# · Busy **67*<num># · Unreachable **62*<num># · All calls **21*<num># · Check *#61# · Cancel all ##002#
