# Build Skill Discovery Questions — 7-Question Template

Developed during the Design Standards and Web Design pack builds (24-26 June 2026). These seven questions go in every build skill's `## Discovery` section, adapted per skill domain.

## Standard template (cinematic-build, spotlight-hero, stitch)

**1. What are we building?** A product page? A launch? A brand moment? One sentence.

**2. Is there already a brand?** Yes (URL or brand guide) or No (describe the vibe in one phrase).

**3. Show me the product or tell me what it looks like.** Photo, sketch, or description.

**4. What style feels right?** Soft and warm / Clean and minimal / Raw and bold / Trustworthy and established / Cinematic and atmospheric. Pick one.

**5. What mood should the images create?** Warm and glowing? Cool and precise? Dark and moody? One word.

**6. Who is this for and what do you want them to feel?** One audience. One feeling.

**7. How do you want to handle the images?** Generate for you or give you prompts to run yourself.

## Per-skill adaptations

- **slide-deck-builder:** Q1 adds "How many slides? What's the story arc?"
- **fly-through-builder:** Q1 becomes "What's the journey? One descent, start to finish."
- **lead-dashboard-builder:** Q3 replaces with "Where does your lead data live?" Q5 replaces with "What's the primary metric?"
- **scroll-journey:** Q1 becomes "What's the story? How many stages?"
- **stitch:** Q1 adds "What sections are we stitching together?"
- **webcam-website:** Q3 becomes "Upload the image you want to transform."
- **real-estate-immersive:** Q1 becomes "What property? Drop a realestate.com.au link." Q3 becomes "Listing link, YouTube walkthrough, or both."

## Design principles

- No technical terms. Plain-English descriptors only.
- Every option is pick-one. User never composes an answer.
- Skill translates answers into locked specs.
- Images get consistency lock: same product, same light, same temperature.
