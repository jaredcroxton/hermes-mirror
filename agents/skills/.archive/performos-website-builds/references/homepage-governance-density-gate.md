# PerformOS homepage governance and density gate

Use when Jared is rebuilding or reviewing the PerformOS homepage after feedback that a previous version felt too full, busy, or overwhelming.

## Core lesson

Do not use the homepage to prove how much PerformOS can do. Use it to help one buyer decide to book a conversation.

The homepage is a decision page, not a catalogue.

## Homepage rule

Every section must pass this test:

> Does this help the buyer decide to book a conversation?

If not, cut it or move it to a detail page.

## Maximum homepage structure

Keep the homepage to seven sections max:

1. Hero: what it is, who it is for, why it matters now, one CTA.
2. Pain: the buyer's current problem, no long explanation.
3. Outcome: what changes after working with PerformOS.
4. Offer: what the buyer actually gets.
5. How it works: three steps only.
6. Proof or examples: short, practical, believable.
7. CTA: book a call, audit, or walkthrough.

## Hard guardrails

- One primary audience.
- One main offer.
- One CTA.
- Homepage max: three offers.
- Hero max: one headline, one subhead, two buttons.
- No product catalogue on the homepage.
- No more than three cards in a row.
- No paragraph over 40 words.
- No section unless it removes doubt or answers one buyer question.
- Anything interesting but not essential goes to a detail page or gets cut.
- No new product added to the homepage unless one old item is removed.

## Detail page model

Homepage = decision page.

Detail pages = proof pages.

Use detail pages for the depth that would make the homepage feel full:

- `/agents.html`
- `/instruments.html`
- `/training.html`
- product-specific proof pages as needed

## Language risk

A sparse design can still fail if the offer is vague. Watch for premium but generic phrases like:

- Powering High Performance.
- We back visionaries.
- Craft ventures that define what comes next.

These may look polished but do not tell the buyer what PerformOS does.

Prefer direct offer language:

- Private AI Teams for Business Leaders.
- Custom AI Agents for Teams That Need Leverage.
- We Build Private AI Systems That Help Businesses Move Faster.

## Three-card naming guidance

If the homepage uses three cards, make each card a clear offer, not a loose category.

Prefer:

- AI Operating Systems: custom dashboards, workflows, and decision tools.
- Private AI Agents: managed agents built around business data, processes, and approvals.
- AI Capability Sprints: 12-week programmes to train teams and embed AI into daily work.

Avoid unfinished labels like `12 weeks Training`.

## Pre-send review questions

Before Jared sends the site to people, ask five reviewers only these questions:

1. What do you think this business does?
2. Who do you think it is for?
3. What would you click next?
4. What confused you?
5. Did it feel too much, too vague, or about right?

If they cannot answer question one in under 10 seconds, the page is not ready.
