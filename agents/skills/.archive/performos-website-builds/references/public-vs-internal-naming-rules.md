# Public vs Internal Naming Rules for PerformOS Website Content

Locked 15 June 2026 after Jared corrected Brock for using internal agent names (Brock, Bob, Lara) on public-facing training and platform pages.

## The rule

**Public-facing website content must use public labels, not internal agent names.**

Internal agent names are for:
- Internal operations documents
- Agent soul files
- Demo walkthroughs (where the viewer has context)
- Internal ops discussions
- Kanban board task assignments

Public labels are for:
- Website copy
- Training pages
- Platform pages
- AntiGravity briefs
- Client proposals
- Any content a new visitor reads

## The training page segmentation

Training is NOT positioned as "AI for business." Training is positioned as skill acquisition for anyone — including sole operators who run their own business and want practical build outcomes.

Three tiers on the training page:
1. **Build Yourself** — for individuals. Personal projects, portfolios, learning AI. No business framing.
2. **Build for Small Business** — for sole operators. Stripe, dashboards, automation, client tools, revenue-generating builds. One person, business outcomes.
3. **Build for Enterprise** — for team leaders. Governed AI, specialist agents, audit trails, secure deployment. Points to the agents page for the done-for-you option.

All three share foundation modules 1 to 7. Modules 8 to 10 fork per tier. The enterprise tier does not replace the agents page — it teaches leaders how to lead AI inside their organisation; the agents page sells the done-for-you build service.

### Design principle: do not split the buyer in half

Two tracks split a single buyer (sole operator who runs a business) into two halves that neither fit. Three tiers gives that buyer a home in the middle. The agents page handles the enterprise sale separately. See `references/training-page-segmentation-model.md` for the full evolution and decision framework.
