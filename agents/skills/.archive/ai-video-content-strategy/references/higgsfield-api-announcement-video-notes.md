# Higgsfield API announcement video notes

Use when turning a current AI video platform announcement into a short founder-style social script.

## Verified anchor points from the 21 September 2026 session

- Higgsfield API pricing page headline: `MODELS API PRICING` with model pricing shown per second.
- Launch page language: `CELEBRATING THE LAUNCH OF HIGGSFIELD API` and `GET UP TO 50% OFF ON 2 VIDEO + 1 IMAGE MODELS`.
- Pricing examples visible on page:
  - Seedance 2.5: from `$0.2057/s`, launch discount from `$0.144/s`.
  - Seedance 2.0: from `$0.1407/s`, launch discount from `$0.0985/s`.
  - Kling 3.0: from `$0.084/s`, launch discount from `$0.042/s`.
- Higgsfield official GitHub repos found:
  - `higgsfield-ai/higgsfield`: Apache-2.0, open-source GPU orchestration and ML training framework. Not the consumer video product UI.
  - `higgsfield-ai/cli`: MIT, Higgsfield CLI for generating images, videos, 3D assets, audio, and analysis from terminal using Higgsfield models.
- `wide-trace/open-higgsfield` is a third-party open-source alternative studio. Do not imply it is Higgsfield's own product unless verified separately.
- News search surfaced Reuters and PRNewswire reporting a $400M raise at a `$5.4B valuation`. Use **valuation**, not evaluation.

## Scripting pattern that worked

For a 30-second talking-head script, target about 85 to 90 words. At a fast social delivery pace, 90 words is roughly 30 seconds.

Best structure:

1. Hook: `Higgsfield just taught the AI market a very big lesson.`
2. Proof: valuation + API access.
3. Consequence: subscriptions lose power.
4. Founder angle: clone/open tooling + build your own version.
5. Punch: generate what you need, pay for what you use.
6. Closing frame: `The future is not another subscription. The future is your own workflow.`

## Pitfalls

- Do not say `$5.4 billion of evaluation`. Say `$5.4 billion valuation`.
- Do not overstate open source. Separate official Higgsfield repositories from third-party alternatives.
- Do not make the video about technical docs. Make it about the business model shift: open tooling, direct access, usage-based pricing.
- Do not bury the script behind a long strategy. Give the script first, then optional visual structure and caption.
