# AI privacy to CREW two-part video pattern

Use this when Jared wants short-form content that links Australian AI/privacy obligations to CREW's local-first positioning.

## Strategy

Make it a two-part authority piece:

1. **Part one creates the problem:** businesses are being taught to connect AI to every app, but not to map personal information movement, automated decision support, or offshore disclosure.
2. **Part two makes CREW the practical answer:** CREW is local-first, controlled access, and can be fully local when paired with local inference.

Do not fearmonger. The tone is: "Most AI builders are missing this."

## Part one, 30 to 45 seconds

Theme: **Your AI workflow may have become a privacy disclosure problem.**

Suggested structure:

- Hook: "Most businesses are being taught AI wrong."
- Beat: "They are being taught how to connect AI to every app they use."
- Beat: "But almost no one is asking where the personal information goes."
- Proof: screenshot the OAIC page showing 10 December 2026.
- Clarifier: "The wording is broader than AI. It can include computer programs, automated tools, and decision-support workflows."
- Close: "If your AI workflow touches personal information, your privacy policy cannot stay pre-AI."

## Part two, 30 to 45 seconds

Theme: **This is why CREW is built around control.**

Suggested structure:

- Hook: "This is why CREW is different."
- Beat: "Most AI tools work by sending your information to someone else's system."
- Beat: "CREW uses a local-first file workflow. Your files stay in approved folders and the AI only works with what it needs for the task."
- Clarifier: "If the model runs locally, that goes further again. Customer data does not need to be sent offshore for inference."
- Close: "CREW is not about chasing shiny AI. It is about building performance tools with control from the start."

## Caption pattern

> Most businesses are being taught to connect AI to every app they use.
>
> But once personal information starts moving through automated tools, the real question becomes: where is the data going, and what decisions is the system helping make?
>
> From 10 December 2026, Australian privacy policies need to say more about certain automated decision-making uses.
>
> This is why local-first handling matters.
>
> AI strategy is not just capability. It is control.

## Lines to avoid

Avoid:

> CREW makes you compliant.

Use:

> CREW is designed to reduce uncontrolled data movement.

Avoid:

> If you use AI, you must disclose every app.

Use:

> If your AI workflow uses personal information, you need to know what data is used, what decisions are supported, and whether anything is disclosed overseas.
