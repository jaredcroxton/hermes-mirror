# Session pattern: street-walk video rebuild

This reference captures a practical pattern from a session where Jared rebuilt a short social video as AI-generated clips with himself as the walking character.

## What worked

- Extracted 50 evenly spaced screenshots from a 36.59 second screen recording.
- Created a structured rebuild folder in Downloads.
- Cropped original vertical frames to remove phone and social UI.
- Created contact sheets for review.
- Created selected moment folders for specific scene beats:
  - temple tap scene
  - temple tap with girl in frame
  - guy looking at girl
  - kid in car
  - restaurant or salt-sprinkle moment
- Opened exact Finder folders/files when Jared needed to drag them into Higgsfield or Seedance.

## What failed

A Higgsfield generation copied the wrong actor and produced a generic man in a blue plaid shirt. The likely cause was too many competing references, including scene frames with other prominent men.

## Corrective pattern

Before building the full scene chain, run a single identity-lock test:

```text
Use only Jared's outdoor street/body photo as the character reference.
Create a 5 second vertical 9:16 realistic handheld street video of Jared walking along a city sidewalk.
Keep the same face, hair, build, navy blazer, and plain white T-shirt.
Do not change him into another man.
```

Only add scene references after identity retention is proven.

## Folder naming pattern

Use direct, human-friendly folders for draggable assets:

```text
TEMPLE_TAP_SCENE_NO_PHONE_UI
TEMPLE_TAP_WITH_GIRL_NO_PHONE_UI
GUY_LOOKING_AT_GIRL_NO_PHONE_UI
KID_IN_CAR_NO_PHONE_UI
RESTAURANT_SALT_SPRINKLE_NO_PHONE_UI
```

## Practical note

If Jared asks for “the black guy with the girl in it,” he is likely asking for the temple-tap side-character frame where the man in a black leather jacket and the woman are both visible. Open the exact file, not the whole source folder where he has to hunt.
