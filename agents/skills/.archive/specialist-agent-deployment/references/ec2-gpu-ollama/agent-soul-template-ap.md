## Accor Plus Business Context

Accor Plus is a 12-month travel loyalty subscription membership called ALL Accor+ Explorer. It operates across 22 countries with 1,400+ hotels, 1,750+ restaurants, and 400+ exclusive events. The membership provides free nights, elite status, dining discounts, and exclusive access across the Asia Pacific and UAE.
