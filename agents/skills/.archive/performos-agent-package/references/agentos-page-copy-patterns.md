# AgentOS page copy patterns

Captured from AgentOS positioning session.

## Product framing

Use:

**AgentOS by PerformOS**

Category:

**Private AI team for business leaders.**

Core idea:

AgentOS is not another AI subscription. It is a managed private AI team built around the client’s roles, workflows, systems, approved sources, and leadership rhythm.

## Hero demo pattern

**Alternative hero line (tested 01 June 2026):**

"Your business does not need another AI subscription. It needs its own private AI team."

This line works as a direct positioning attack on SaaS AI fatigue. Use it as a hero headline or opening statement when the audience is business leaders who have already tried ChatGPT/Claude subscriptions and found them insufficient for serious business work.

When creating a hero or first "how does this work?" section, do not show a generic chatbot or a simple admin task.

Show the operating loop:

**Ask → Review → Deliver → Clarify → Build**

Recommended structure:

1. Compact card opens into a full-size chat box inside the hero or demo section.
2. Operator asks a messy business question.
3. AgentOS reviews approved business context.
4. AgentOS delivers a structured output, not just a chat reply.
5. AgentOS asks the intelligent next question before building or automating.

Example:

**Operator asks**

“We need more qualified leads this quarter without wasting budget. Where should I focus first?”

**AgentOS reviews**

- approved sources
- current channels
- lead quality
- conversion patterns
- owner capacity

**AgentOS delivers**

“A focused growth brief showing where to focus, what to stop, and the weekly actions most likely to convert into revenue.”

**AgentOS asks**

“Before I build the dashboard, what counts as a qualified lead for this business?”

Scope line:

**The same loop works for meeting prep, decision briefs, risk reviews, people conversations, operations updates, and dashboards.**

## Pricing page pattern

Do not lead with parameter counts.

Lead with business capability:

- **Standard:** fast, practical AI agents for everyday business support.
- **Advanced:** higher-capability AI agents for complex business reasoning.

Both tiers include up to 10 custom agents. The difference is reasoning depth, not agent count.

Current pricing model:

- Standard: $4,999 AUD/month + $3,000 setup
- Advanced: $4,999 AUD/month + $6,000 setup
- Six-month minimum engagement

Technical explanation:

- Standard uses 8B models for everyday business tasks.
- Advanced uses 14B to 70B models for complex reasoning, strategy, ambiguity, risk review, and deeper synthesis.

Pricing page sections:

1. Hero: Private AI team for business leaders
2. Choose your reasoning level
3. Standard vs Advanced pricing cards
4. Every plan includes
5. Comparison table
6. Which tier is right for you?
7. Setup fee explanation
8. FAQ

## Security page pattern

Lead with:

**Private by default. Governed like infrastructure.**

Or:

**Bring AI inside your boundary.**

Core claim:

AgentOS lets businesses use AI without making public AI tools the place where sensitive work happens.

Avoid saying:

“Your data can never be interpreted by any language model.”

It is technically wrong because approved models interpret business context to help the user.

Use instead:

**Your business data does not need to be sent to public AI tools or used to train vendor models.**

Security pillars:

- controlled infrastructure
- approved sources
- role-scoped access
- human approval
- audit logs
- no public AI tools by default
- no vendor model training on business data in local/private configurations
- clear offboarding and wipe process

Page structure:

1. Hero: Private by default. Governed like infrastructure.
2. Problem: The risk is not AI. The risk is uncontrolled AI.
3. Boundary: Your boundary comes first.
4. Security pillars.
5. Local/private model.
6. Public AI tools vs AgentOS comparison.
7. Governance workflow.
8. CTA: Bring AI inside your boundary.

## Solution page pattern

The solution page should not feel like a simple list of agents. It should sell:

**Your operating model, encoded.**

Two solution paths:

### 1. Orchestrated company AI team

Up to 10 specialist agents, each playing a defined role across the business.

Best for shared AI capability across leadership, operations, growth, HR, research, reporting, and workflow support.

Example agents:

- CEO Advisor
- Chief of Staff
- Growth Advisor
- HR and Compliance Advisor
- Learning Designer
- Operations Advisor
- Research and Synthesis Agent
- Report Writer
- Customer Insight Agent
- Workflow Builder

### 2. Personalised leader and team agents

Agents configured around specific executives, leaders, departments, or teams.

Best for executive support, team operating rhythm, work-style personalisation, meeting prep, decision support, and follow-up discipline.

Core line:

**AgentOS is built around the business, not the other way around.**

Recommended combined page structure:

1. Hero: One private AI team. Built around how your business actually works.
2. Two cards: Company AI team and Personalised leader agents.
3. Explanation: Your operating model, encoded.
4. Use cases: Leaders, Teams, Operations.
5. Conversation animation: messy request to useful output.
6. How it works: Map, Design, Build, Pilot, Refine.
7. Security bridge: Built inside your approved boundary.
8. CTA: Book an AgentOS team assessment.

## Copy discipline

Use executive/commercial examples in hero/demo sections. Avoid making invoice chasing or basic admin the lead example. Put admin examples lower down.

Hero/demo examples should show high-value work:

- revenue focus
- risk review
- meeting preparation
- priority setting
- people conversations
- operating visibility
- dashboard build from a business question

Do not over-explain Google Chat in top-level copy. Google Chat is the doorway. AgentOS is the value.
