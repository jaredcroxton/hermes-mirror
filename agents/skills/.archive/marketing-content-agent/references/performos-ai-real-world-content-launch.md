# PerformOS AI real-world content launch pattern

Use this reference when Jared asks for a content strategy to launch PerformOS, AgentOS, or the private AI team offer using real-life video, street interviews, founder videos, or social-first content.

## Strategic wedge

Do not launch with generic "AI is amazing" content. The stronger PerformOS wedge is:

> AI is confusing, risky, overhyped, and most businesses do not know where to start. PerformOS makes it practical.

Core positioning:

> PerformOS helps business leaders turn AI from noise into working business capability.

Lead with operational pain, leadership uncertainty, safe adoption, real workflows, private AI teams, and practical implementation. Do not lead with tools, chatbots, productivity hacks, or abstract AI hype.

## Big creative idea

Campaign frame: **AI in the Real World**

Short-form street, workplace, and founder-style content showing the gap between AI hype and what real people actually struggle with.

The street content is both marketing and market research. It captures the exact language people use about AI confusion, trust, workflow pain, and adoption risk.

## Hero series: AI on the Street

Format:
- 20 to 45 second clips
- One question only
- Real person response
- PerformOS insight at the end
- Calm, respectful tone. Never mock the interviewee.

Question bank:
- What is one thing about AI at work that still confuses you?
- If AI could take one task off your plate tomorrow, what would it be?
- Do you trust AI with your business data?
- What worries you most about using AI in your business?
- Do you think your company has an AI strategy?
- What is the most boring part of your job?
- Would you trust AI to write to your customers?
- Do you know what your staff are using ChatGPT for?
- What would make you feel safer using AI at work?

Closing line pattern:

> This is the real problem. Most teams are not anti-AI. They are unclear on where it fits. PerformOS starts there.

## Supporting content pillars

1. **Founder field notes**
   - Jared direct to camera.
   - Operator-led insight, not polished corporate theatre.
   - Topics: workflow-first AI, safe adoption, private agents, staff already using AI, AI as an operating decision.

2. **From Messy to Managed**
   - Before-and-after workflow breakdowns.
   - Examples: recruitment no-shows, customer follow-up, manager coaching, training content, meeting notes, policy Q&A, lead qualification, sales call coaching, onboarding, churn signals.
   - Structure: messy workflow → why it breaks → what AI should and should not do → PerformOS way → CTA.

3. **Safe AI for Real Businesses**
   - Trust and risk content.
   - Topics: customer data, private AI teams, human approval gates, low-risk first workflows, what should never be automated.
   - Principle: PerformOS should lead with judgement, not speed.

4. **Meet the Private AI Team**
   - Scenario-led AgentOS demos.
   - Use sparingly at launch. Too much product demo too early feels like software sales.
   - Frame AgentOS according to actual product state. Do not imply broad deployment if not ready.

## Campaign concept: The AI Gap

Core line:

> The AI gap is not between people using AI and people not using AI. It is between businesses experimenting randomly and businesses building capability deliberately.

Use this across homepage copy, LinkedIn posts, lead magnets, webinars, and video captions.

## Launch funnel

Top of funnel:
- Street interviews
- Founder field notes
- Myth-busting clips
- Reaction videos to AI hype
- Real business problem clips

Middle of funnel:
- AI Readiness Checklist
- 10 workflows every business should review before buying AI tools
- Private AI team vs chatbot guide
- Safe AI adoption framework
- Workflow breakdowns

Bottom of funnel:
- AI Readiness Audit
- AI Workflow Sprint
- AgentOS by PerformOS

## Website role

The PerformOS website should do four jobs:
1. Say who it is for: business leaders who know AI matters but do not want random tools, risk, or hype.
2. Name the problem: AI is already entering the business but most teams lack workflows, boundaries, and ownership.
3. Explain the method: Assess. Design. Train. Build. Support.
4. Convert with one primary CTA: **Book an AI clarity call**. Secondary CTA: **Download the AI readiness checklist**.

Do not make the homepage explain every product. Lead with the transformation problem. Put AgentOS as the flagship solution behind it.

## Weekly cadence

Minimum viable launch cadence:
- Three street interview clips
- Two founder insight clips
- One workflow breakdown
- One trust or risk clip
- One AgentOS scenario demo

This produces seven pieces per week. For 90 days: 84 short videos, 12 LinkedIn longer posts, 12 email pieces, three lead magnets, and one webinar or live session per month.

## Production model

Monthly half-day shoot:
- 20 street interviews
- 10 founder direct-to-camera clips
- Five workflow explainers
- Five B-roll scenes

Expected output: 40 to 60 clips.

Visual style:
- Premium but not overproduced
- Captions, sharp cuts, clean lower thirds, warm colour grade, subtle PerformOS branding
- Avoid generic AI visuals: robot hands, glowing brains, cyberpunk effects, neon AI clichés

## Consent and reputation guardrails

For street videos:
- Ask permission before recording
- Explain brand use
- Get written consent via phone form
- Avoid sensitive workplace details
- Avoid filming children
- Do not bait people into looking uninformed
- Do not mock people’s answers

Tone principle:

> Real people are asking the right questions.

Never frame the series as "look how confused people are about AI."

## Channel strategy

LinkedIn is the primary B2B trust channel. Use TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts for reach and language testing. Use email for weekly authority building through **The Practical AI Brief**.
