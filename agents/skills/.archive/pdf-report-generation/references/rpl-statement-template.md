# RPL Statement Template

For ECU Recognition of Prior Learning applications in the Master of Business Psychology.

## Structure

```
# Recognition of Prior Learning Statement

Student: [Full Name]
Student Number: [ECU ID]
Course: Master of Business Psychology (T84)
Date: [DD Month YYYY]

## Basis of Application

[2-3 sentence summary of the professional experience the RPL is based on.]

This statement has been reviewed and verified by [Verifier Name], [Title], [Organisation].

---

# [UNIT CODE] [Unit Title]

## Learning Outcome 1

[Heading: the exact learning outcome text from the unit outline]

[200-400 word paragraph mapping specific professional experience to this outcome.
Do NOT just describe what you did. Explain HOW the experience demonstrates the outcome.
Reference named frameworks where applicable (Kotter, ADKAR, GROW, COM-B, etc.).
Use concrete examples: market names, team sizes, timeframes, specific decisions.]

## Learning Outcome 2

...

[Repeat for every learning outcome in the unit outline.]

---

# [SECOND UNIT CODE] [Second Unit Title]

[Same structure as above.]

---

## Summary of Professional Evidence

The professional experience described in this statement spans:

- [Bullet list of key experience dimensions]

This statement has been reviewed and verified by:

[Verifier Name]
[Title]
[Organisation]

[Signature on file]

---

**Prepared by:** [Full Name]
**Date:** [DD Month YYYY]
```

## Rules

- Address every learning outcome individually under its own heading.
- Minimum one paragraph (200+ words) per learning outcome.
- Map experience to outcomes, not just activity description.
- Reference named frameworks where relevant.
- Include a note if unit codes have changed across years.
- Name the verifier and their role.
- Include student name, number, and course.
- Zero em dashes.
- Formal academic tone; no internal shorthand or agent names.
- The markdown source is the source of truth. PDF is rendered from it.

## Common pitfalls

- Describing activity without linking to the specific learning outcome (e.g. "I led a change program" is insufficient; must add "which demonstrates the ability to justify a phased change strategy because...").
- Using old unit codes without noting the change to current codes.
- Missing the verifier details.
- Paragraphs that are too short (under 150 words) and lack sufficient depth.
