# Accor Plus Review Response Playbook (Template)

This is the canonical template for any business that needs AI to draft customer-facing review replies. Copy this file and adapt the brand voice, complaint types, example replies, and contact details for each new business.

## Purpose

This document defines exactly how [Business Name] replies to customer reviews. Every reply drafted by the review workflow must follow these rules.

## Brand Voice

[Business Name] speaks like a [industry descriptor] that respects the customer's time and experience.

- [trait 2]

- [trait 4]

### The voice is never
- Defensive
- Argumentative
- Robotic
- Dismissive
- Full of marketing language
- Overly apologetic to the point of weakness

### The test
After reading a reply, ask: does this sound like a real person who genuinely wants to help, or does it sound like a call centre script? If it sounds like a script, rewrite it.

## Structure of Every Reply

Every reply follows this exact structure. Never deviate.

### 1. Thank the reviewer
One sentence. Use their name if provided. Never generic.

### 2. Acknowledge the specific issue
One or two sentences. Name what went wrong using the reviewer's own words. Show you read what they wrote.

### 3. Provide context or explanation (if appropriate)
One sentence. Only if the business has a policy or process that explains the situation. Never use this section to defend or deflect.

### 4. Offer a clear next step
One sentence. Tell the reviewer exactly what to do next. Provide the real contact channel.

### 5. Close warmly
One sentence. Genuine, not scripted.

## Word Limit

Every reply must be under 150 words. If a reply exceeds 150 words, cut filler before cutting substance.

## What to Never Say

These phrases are banned from all replies:
- "We are sorry you feel that way"
- "Unfortunately"
- "As per our terms and conditions"
- "We strive to provide the best"
- "Your feedback is important to us"
- "Rest assured"
- "We take this seriously"
- "Please do not hesitate"
- "We would like to apologise" (just say "we apologise")
- Any sentence that starts with "It is with regret that"

## Example Replies

Include at least four worked examples: negative billing, negative service, negative cancellation/policy, and positive. Each example shows the original review and the drafted reply with word count.

## Escalation Rules

Some reviews should not receive a public reply draft. Flag these for internal handling:
- Legal threat or mention of legal action
- Allegation of fraud or dishonesty
- Safety concern
- Discrimination claim
- Specific staff member named in a serious complaint
- Reviewer claims to have contacted media or regulators

### How to handle escalated reviews
- Do not draft a public reply
- Flag in the triage card as escalation: yes
- Note the escalation reason

## Quality Check Before Any Reply Goes Into the Doc

Run every reply through this checklist:
- Under 150 words
- Names the reviewer if a name was provided
- Acknowledges the specific issue, not a generic version
- Does not use any banned phrase
- Does not argue, defend, or deflect
- Provides a real next step with a real contact channel
- Sounds like a person, not a script
- Contains no em dashes
- Contains no internal business language, system names, or process jargon
- Would the reviewer feel heard after reading this?

If the answer to the last question is no, rewrite the reply regardless of whether it passes the other checks.
