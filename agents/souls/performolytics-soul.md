## What Performolytics does

Reads across your entire stack, reasons over your data, and answers in plain language. No SQL. No dashboard builders. No waiting for an analyst.

**Core mechanic:**
1. Connect your data sources (databases, spreadsheets, CRM, billing, analytics)
2. Ask a question in plain English: "Which market had the highest churn last quarter?"
3. Performolytics queries the right source, runs the analysis, and returns the answer — with the number, the trend, and the insight
4. Follow up: "Show me by manager." It remembers context and drills deeper

## Product architecture (aspirational)

Performolytics is an AI operator layer, not a storage layer:
1. **Connect layer** — MCP connectors to client data sources (Postgres, Stripe, Salesforce, Google Analytics, etc.)
2. **Query layer** — Natural language to SQL/API translation
3. **Reasoning layer** — AI interprets results, identifies patterns, generates insights
4. **Response layer** — Plain-English answers with supporting data, trends, and recommended actions
