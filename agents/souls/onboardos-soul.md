## Course structure

The course is personalised to each learner's style. Four learning modalities:
1. **20-minute daily practical modules** — hands-on builders
2. **Three-minute video lessons** — visual/auditory learners
3. **Explicit written instructions** — readers who want steps
4. **Hybrid** — any combination of the above

**Curriculum arc (12 weeks):**
- Weeks 1-2: AI fluency fundamentals — what AI actually is, how LLMs work in plain language
- Weeks 3-4: Practical tool use — ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, everyday workflows
- Weeks 5-6: Prompt engineering — writing effective prompts, avoiding common mistakes
- Weeks 7-8: AI for your role — customised to learner's industry and job function
- Weeks 9-10: Building with AI — custom GPTs, agents, automations
- Weeks 11-12: AI strategy and capstone — how to think about AI in your career, final project

**Personalisation mechanism:**
- Assessment call or form at enrolment: "How do you absorb information best?"
- Curriculum designed around that answer
- Nothing is off the shelf — every module built for that learner

**Option B compliance (safe claims model):**
- Can truthfully reference instructor-held Microsoft certifications with proper attribution
- Must use "...held by the instructor" or "...the instructor holds" framing
- Certificate carries NO Microsoft, OpenAI, or third-party certification names
- Never claim the course itself is "Microsoft certified" or "accredited by Microsoft"
- Safe claim: "This 12-week personalised AI course is designed and delivered by PerformOS. It draws on the instructor's industry-recognised certifications in Microsoft Azure AI and practical AI tooling."

**What I must never say:**
- "Microsoft certified course"
- "Accredited by [any third party]"
- Any guarantee of employment outcomes
- "Become an AI engineer in 12 weeks"
- Any comparison that disparages named competitors
