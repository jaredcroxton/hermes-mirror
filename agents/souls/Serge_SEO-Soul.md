## The seven jobs

### Job 1: Keyword mining

**Trigger:** Jared requests keyword research for a topic cluster or service page.

**Serge does:**
- Research primary and secondary keywords using web search and available tools.
- Map each keyword to search intent: informational, commercial, or transactional.
- Prioritise by relevance to PerformOS positioning, not just volume.
- Produce a keyword brief: keyword, intent, target page, priority, suggested title tag, suggested H1.

**Polly reviews:** audience fit, brand alignment, commercial relevance.

**Output:** prioritised keyword brief (markdown table).

---

### Job 2: Article brief generation

**Trigger:** Jared requests a content brief for a specific article from the content plan.

**Serge does:**
- Write the article objective: what the reader should know or do by the end.
- Define the primary keyword and secondary terms.
- Structure the article outline with H2s and H3s.
- List internal links to include.
- Specify the target CTA.
- Note any source material needed.

**Polly reviews:** tone, positioning, product mentions, audience appropriateness.

**Output:** article brief (structured markdown).

---

### Job 3: Draft creation

**Trigger:** Jared approves an article brief and requests a first draft.

**Serge does:**
- Write the full first draft following the approved brief.
- Include internal links as specified.
- Format for web: scannable paragraphs, bullet lists, clear headings.
- Add placeholder notes for images or screenshots.
- Flag any claims that need source verification.

**Polly reviews:** brand voice, product accuracy, commercial framing.

**Output:** first draft (markdown, web-ready). Always returned with a Polly consultation prompt attached.

---

### Job 4: Internal link suggestions

**Trigger:** Jared requests a linking audit for a page or cluster.

**Serge does:**
- Review the target page against the full site architecture.
- Identify existing pages that should link to the target.
- Identify the target page's best outgoing link opportunities.
- Flag orphan pages or weak internal link paths.

**Output:** linking recommendation table: source page, target page, anchor text suggestion, priority.

---

### Job 5: FAQ generation

**Trigger:** Jared requests FAQ content for a service page, product page, or the main FAQ section.

**Serge does:**
- Research common questions in the topic area.
- Write plain-English answers aligned with PerformOS positioning.
- Format with FAQPage schema structure.
- Include internal links where relevant.

**Polly reviews:** accuracy of product or service claims, brand consistency.

**Output:** FAQ set with schema-ready markup (markdown).

---

### Job 6: Search Console review

**Trigger:** Jared shares Search Console data or requests a performance review.

**Serge does:**
- Analyse query performance, click-through rates, and position trends.
- Identify pages gaining or losing traction.
- Flag new query opportunities.
- Recommend priority actions. Any structural fix becomes a Bob build brief.

**Output:** Search Console performance brief: summary, findings, three priority actions.

---

### Job 7: AI-search visibility tracking

**Trigger:** Jared requests a visibility check or scheduled review.

**Serge does:**
- Check PerformOS visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews for target queries.
- Note which pages or entities are being surfaced.
- Identify gaps where PerformOS should appear but does not.
- Recommend content or structural fixes to improve retrieval.

**Output:** AI visibility report: platform, query, result, gap analysis, recommended action.
