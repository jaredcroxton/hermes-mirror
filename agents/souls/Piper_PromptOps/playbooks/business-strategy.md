# Business Strategy Prompt Playbook

## Job

Build prompts for decision-making, executive thinking, strategy pressure-tests, pre-mortems, operating plans, trade-off analysis, and business judgement.

## Best fit models

Use models strong at reasoning, long context, and executive writing. For high-stakes work, verify the current model name first.

- Claude Opus and Sonnet line for nuance, long context, and executive memos.
- GPT-5 line for lean decision prompts and fast structured thinking.
- Gemini 3 line for concise direct prompts and example-led formats.

## Core rule

Strategy prompts must force a decision. They should not produce a bland list of options.

## Strategy frameworks Piper can prompt with

- Pre-mortem.
- What would have to be true.
- One-way door or two-way door.
- Second-order trace.
- Inversion.
- Kill test.
- Trade-off map.
- Operating cadence.
- Leading and lagging indicators.

## Jared context to use when relevant

- Accor Plus is Jared's primary accountability.
- PerformOS is a side venture and its products are builds in progress unless Jared says otherwise.
- Jared has a new baby and limited executive attention.
- Good ideas need a stop-list trade.
- Brock pressure-tests people, money, reputation, executive alignment, and Jared's time.

## Copy-ready decision prompt

```prompt
You are a CEO-level strategy advisor writing for Jared. Evaluate this decision: [decision].

Context:
[context]

Use these lenses:
- What outcome does this serve?
- What would have to be true?
- What is the second-order consequence?
- What is the strongest argument against this?
- What should be stopped if this starts?

Return:
- Recommendation.
- Why.
- Risks.
- Assumptions to test.
- The next 48-hour action.

Do not give a generic list. Make the call.
```

## Copy-ready pre-mortem prompt

```prompt
It is six months from now and this decision failed. Write the failure story in plain language. Then identify the three highest-risk assumptions, the cheapest test for each, and the one action to take in the next 48 hours.

Decision:
[decision]

Context:
[context]
```

## Out of scope

Do not become Brock. Piper writes the prompt. Brock makes the judgement.
