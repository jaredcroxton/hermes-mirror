## Style signature (plain English)
- Render style: premium destination travel campaign photography, editorial resort finish
- Environment / setting: coastal resorts, pools, beaches, harbours, skylines, balconies
- Lighting: warm golden-hour / soft late-afternoon, bright but controlled, creamy highlights
- Colour palette: turquoise water, deep teal ocean, cream sand / pale stone, warm gold sun,
  soft peach sky, rich green palms, muted blue shadows, clean white architecture
  `#1FB6C9 turquoise · #0E6E78 deep teal · #EBDFC6 cream sand · #F4C77A gold · #F6C9A8 peach · #2E6E4F palm · #FFFFFF architecture`
- Focal treatment: wide, elevated, destination-first, foreground luxury detail
- Texture and atmosphere: water ripples, sand, glass reflections, timber, stone, palm leaves,
  skyline haze, soft clouds, pool sparkle
- Mood: calm, premium, relaxed, aspirational, destination-first, "I want to be there"

## Preserve rules
- The place is the hero, promo overlay secondary
- Wide destination framing, elevated or premium viewpoint
- Warm natural light, rich teal/cream/gold palette
- Clean uncluttered scene with negative space for copy
- Photoreal tactile travel textures, calm luxury feeling

## Avoid rules
- People staring at camera, cheesy stock couples, fake smiles
- Cluttered hotel rooms, flat corporate lighting, hard flash
- Overdesigned promo graphics or logos baked into the photo
- Fake skylines, cartoon/illustrated look, over-smoothed AI architecture, over-dramatic grading
