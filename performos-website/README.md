# PerformOS 12-Week AI Course — Complete Build Package

## What this is

The full build package for the new PerformOS 12-week personalised AI course page. Everything you need to understand, verify, and deploy.

## What happened before this

1. **Outlier research** — Analysed 8 top AI course/education homepages (DeepLearning.AI, Coursera, Maven, Dataquest, Udacity, Fast.ai, Google, Section) to reverse-engineer what winning course pages actually do.
2. **Legal governance** — Atticus_Counsel produced a full legal analysis. Atticus_Governance produced the Option B operational pack. Option B = instructor-credential framing with mandatory disclaimers.
3. **Page rebuilt** — The course.html was rebuilt as a 15-beat page informed by research and fully Option B compliant.

## Option B compliance at a glance

- Instructor credentials in body text only — never in hero H1
- No Microsoft, Azure, OpenAI, or ChatGPT logos anywhere
- No third-party certification names on the Certificate of Completion
- Mandatory negative disclosure on the page
- Trademark attribution in footer
- PerformOS brand always more prominent than any third-party mark
