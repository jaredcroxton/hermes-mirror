# Page Structure — 15-Beat Layout

The PerformOS 12-week AI course page follows a 15-beat structure. Each beat has a specific job. Nothing is decorative — every section earns its place.

---

## Beat 1: Navigation

**Purpose:** Standard site nav. Unchanged from existing site.

**Elements:** PerformOS logo/brand lockup, nav links (12-Week Course, Catalogue, Transformation, Implementation, Workshop, Studio), "Start your plan" + "Contact" CTAs.

**Option B note:** No third-party marks in nav. PerformOS brand is the most prominent element.

---

## Beat 2: Hero — "Your personal AI tutor"

**Purpose:** Primary conversion. Make the personalisation promise immediately. Show the price.

**Headline:** "Your personal AI tutor. 12 weeks. Career-ready."
**Eyebrow:** "12 Weeks · Personalised · $499 One-Time"
**Subheadline:** "A course that adapts to your skill level, your pace, and your goals. Not a crowded classroom. Not a one-size-fits-all curriculum. Just you, an expert instructor, and the AI skills employers actually want."
**Supporting copy:** Existing intro paragraph about not being the average learner.
**CTA:** "Book your plan — $499" (primary) + "Compare options" (secondary)

**Research basis:** Hero sections are universal (8/8 sites). But NO competitor puts price in the hero. This is deliberate differentiation.

**Option B note:** Zero third-party marks in hero. "Personalised" is the hook, not certifications.

---

## Beat 3: Trust Ribbon

**Purpose:** Immediate social proof substitution. Since PerformOS doesn't have partner logos like DeepLearning.AI, use key stats.

**Elements:** 4 stat cards in a row:
- "12 — Weeks, fully personalised"
- "1-on-1 — Instructor-led, not self-serve"
- "$499 — One-time. No subscription."
- "✓ — Certificate of Completion"

**Research basis:** Social proof stats appear in 7/8 top sites. Dataquest uses "TRUSTED BY 100,000+ LEARNERS." We adapted the pattern for a new provider.

---

## Beat 4: The Problem — "Most AI courses treat you like everyone else"

**Purpose:** Pre-qualify visitors. Name the pain. Make the solution credible by contrast.

**Headline:** "Most AI courses treat you like everyone else."
**Format:** Side-by-side comparison cards:
- Left: "What most courses do" — generic, self-paced, no feedback, irrelevant content
- Right: "What PerformOS does" — custom, 1-on-1, real feedback, role-relevant

**Research basis:** Problem/solution contrast is a proven pattern in DTC and SaaS. Not from the 8-site AI dataset directly — from broader Outlier pattern recognition.

---

## Beat 5: How You Learn

**Purpose:** Explain the personalisation mechanism — the format, not the content.

**Headline:** "Choose how you learn. We build around it."
**Format:** 4 cards:
- 20-minute daily sprints
- 3-minute video lessons
- Explicit written instructions
- Mixed format

**Note:** This section was retained from the original page. It's a strong differentiator.

---

## Beat 6: How It Works

**Purpose:** Walk through the customer journey. De-risk an unfamiliar format.

**Headline:** "Four steps. Twelve weeks. One outcome."
**Format:** 4 step cards:
- 01 Assess — "We talk" (conversation, not a form)
- 02 Design — "We build" (custom curriculum)
- 03 Delivery — "You learn" (your format, your pace)
- 04 Capable — "You ship" (portfolio project + certificate)

**Research basis:** How It Works sections appear in 4/8 sites. Important for an unfamiliar value proposition like personalised learning.

---

## Beat 7: Instructor — "Learn from a certified AI practitioner"

**Purpose:** THE trust anchor. For an independent provider, the instructor IS the brand.

**Headline:** "Learn from a certified AI practitioner."
**Body:** "Your instructor, Jared Croxton, holds current Microsoft Azure AI certifications — AI-900: Azure AI Fundamentals and AI-102: Azure AI Engineer Associate. These credentials inform the course curriculum and practical exercises. Real certifications. Real teaching. Not a chatbot."
**Format:** 3 cards:
- AI-900: Azure AI Fundamentals
- AI-102: Azure AI Engineer
- Practitioner: Real-world experience

**Inline disclaimer:** "Microsoft and Azure are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies. PerformOS is an independent training provider."

**Research basis:** Instructor sections in 6/8 sites. But most underutilise them — they're an afterthought. We made the instructor the centrepiece.

**Option B compliance:** Certifications in body text, not hero. Inline disclaimer present. PerformOS brand more prominent. No logos or icons.

---

## Beat 8: Curriculum Overview

**Purpose:** Show the product. Make personalisation visible inside the curriculum structure.

**Headline:** "What you will learn in 12 weeks."
**Format:** 4 phase cards:
- Weeks 1-3: AI Foundations & Prompt Engineering
- Weeks 4-6: Building with AI (agents, RAG, APIs)
- Weeks 7-9: Your Specialisation Track (Marketing/Engineering/Leadership AI)
- Weeks 10-12: Capstone Project & Career

**Research basis:** Curriculum sections in 7/8 sites. The specialisation track is the personalisation signal — no competitor shows this.

---

## Beat 9: Certificate of Completion

**Purpose:** Tangible outcome. What they walk away with.

**Headline:** "Graduate with proof you can share."
**Format:** Certificate mockup in a card, plus bullet points about sharing on LinkedIn, adding to CV.
**Mockup text:** Clean PerformOS-only wording (see `legal/03-certificate-rules.md`).
**Disclaimer:** "This certificate is issued by PerformOS and is not a Microsoft, OpenAI, or third-party certification."

**Research basis:** "Get certified" is a standalone step in Dataquest's 5-step How It Works. Certificates are the #1 tangible outcome — feature them prominently.

**Option B compliance:** No third-party marks on the certificate. Disclaimer present.

---

## Beat 10: Spectrum — "Start anywhere"

**Purpose:** Show the course works for multiple entry points.

**Headline:** "Start anywhere. Go as far as you want."
**Format:** 2 cards:
- Starting from zero (never used AI beyond ChatGPT)
- Ready to build (already use AI, want to build agents/automation)

**Note:** Retained from original page. Good for widening the funnel.

---

## Beat 11: Pricing — "One price. Everything included."

**Purpose:** Transparent pricing as differentiation.

**Format:** Large "$499" + detail card with inclusions list:
- Skills assessment and goal-setting conversation
- Custom 12-week curriculum in your format
- 1-on-1 instructor check-ins
- Specialisation track
- Capstone portfolio project
- PerformOS Certificate of Completion
- Lifetime access to course materials
- One-time payment. Owned forever.

**Research basis:** Only 1/8 sites shows pricing. Transparency IS the differentiator.

---

## Beat 12: Mandatory Negative Disclosure

**Purpose:** Legal requirement under Option B. Protects against ACL overall-impression challenge.

**Format:** Full disclosure block in a highlighted card:
"This course is not a Microsoft, OpenAI, or ChatGPT certification program. Completion does not confer any Microsoft, OpenAI, or third-party credential. PerformOS is an independent training provider..."

**Option B compliance:** This is the single most important legal element on the page. Do not remove. Do not shrink to fine print.

---

## Beat 13: Comparison Table

**Purpose:** Competitive positioning. Show value vs alternatives.

**Format:** Table comparing PerformOS vs Academy Xi, AIM, and online courses across:
- Price ($499 vs $899-$3,150 vs $600-$800 vs $15-59/mo)
- Duration (12 weeks vs 6-12 months vs 1-2 days vs self-paced)
- Personalised (100% custom vs some mentoring vs off the shelf vs none)
- Learning format (your choice vs various)
- Instructor (Microsoft-certified vs varies)
- Subscription (none vs varies)
- Built for (individuals who feel behind vs various)

**Note:** Retained and enhanced from original page. Added Instructor row.

---

## Beat 14: FAQ

**Purpose:** De-risk the purchase. Answer objections before they become reasons not to buy.

**7 questions:**
1. How is the course personalised to how I learn?
2. What can I expect to be able to do after 12 weeks?
3. Is this a Microsoft-certified course? ← Option B buyer objection handler
4. Why doesn't the certificate say Microsoft or Azure? ← Option B buyer objection handler
5. Is there a team version?
6. How does this compare to Academy Xi or AIM?
7. Can I switch format partway through?

**Research basis:** FAQ in 3/8 sites. The two Option B objection handlers are unique to this page.

---

## Beat 15: Footer

**Purpose:** Standard footer with trademark attribution.

**Elements:**
- PerformOS brand
- Catalogue links, Services links, Studio links
- Copyright
- Full trademark attribution: "Microsoft, Azure, and Microsoft Azure are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies. OpenAI and ChatGPT are trademarks of OpenAI. All other trademarks are the property of their respective owners. Use of these marks does not imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation. PerformOS is an independent training provider."

**Option B compliance:** Trademark attribution in footer. Required.
