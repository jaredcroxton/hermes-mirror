# Key Findings — What Shaped the Build

Three findings from analysing 8 top AI course homepages drove every decision in the new PerformOS course page.

---

## Finding 1: Almost Nobody Shows Pricing on the Homepage

Only 1 of 8 sites displayed pricing information. Every major platform (DeepLearning.AI, Maven, Dataquest, Udacity, Coursera) hides pricing behind "Start Free" or "Enroll Now" CTAs.

**Implication for PerformOS:** For a transparent $499 fixed-price course, showing price prominently IS differentiation. It signals confidence and no bait-and-switch.

**What we did:** Put the price in the hero eyebrow, in the trust ribbon, in the pricing section, and in every CTA. "$499" appears five times on the page.

---

## Finding 2: Instructor Credentials Are Underutilised

Only DeepLearning.AI (Andrew Ng's celebrity status) and Udacity give instructor presentation real estate. Most sites treat the instructor as secondary to the platform brand. For an independent provider, this is an opportunity.

**Implication for PerformOS:** The instructor's Microsoft AI-900/AI-102 certifications are a differentiator. Most marketplaces say "taught by Google/IBM" — rarely "taught by a certified Microsoft AI engineer with real-world deployment experience."

**What we did:** Dedicated instructor section (Beat 6) with three cards for AI-900, AI-102, and real-world experience. Certifications are framed as instructor credentials, not course features (Option B compliance).

---

## Finding 3: "Personalisation" Is a Vacant Positioning

No site analysed sells "personalised" learning as its core differentiator. Dataquest comes closest with "Find your path" (quiz-based) but nobody says: "This course adapts to YOU."

**Implication for PerformOS:** "Personalised AI Course" is a blue-ocean claim. If PerformOS truly delivers personalisation, this is the homepage's single most important headline concept.

**What we did:** Made personalisation the hero headline: "Your personal AI tutor. 12 weeks. Career-ready." Every subsequent section reinforces the personalisation mechanism — learning styles, custom curriculum, specialisation tracks, instructor feedback.

---

## Bonus finding: The Problem/Solution contrast pattern

Several high-performing sites (AiSDR, and patterns observed in DTC/SaaS) use a "What most [competitors] do vs What we do" side-by-side contrast early on the page. This pre-qualifies visitors and makes the solution more credible.

**What we did:** Beat 3 — "Most AI courses treat you like everyone else" with a left/right comparison of generic courses vs PerformOS. Not from the 8-site dataset directly, but from the broader Outlier pattern recognition.
