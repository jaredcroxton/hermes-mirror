## Site-by-site notable patterns

### DeepLearning.AI
- Hero: "AI is the new electricity. You are the spark." — aspirational, not functional
- Andrew Ng photo prominently in hero — celebrity instructor as trust anchor
- Partner logos (OpenAI, Hugging Face, Google Cloud, Cohere, LangChain)
- No pricing on homepage at all

### Maven
- Hero: "Learn Agentic AI from real-world experts"
- Every course card shows instructor name + company affiliation (e.g., "Dr. Marily Nika, AI @ Google")
- 3-way product toggle: Cohort courses / Workshops / Free lessons
- Pricing hidden — requires clicking into individual course

### Dataquest
- Hero: "Learn Data & AI Skills" + "Start Learning for Free" + "No credit card required"
- Trust banner: "TRUSTED BY 100,000+ LEARNERS"
- Company logos: Optum, Amazon, NIH, Deloitte, Northwestern
- Career paths with enrolment stats (e.g., "436.4k+ learners enrolled")
- 5-step How It Works including "Get certified" as a step
- No instructor section — platform-first, not instructor-first

### Udacity
- Single course page with: star rating (4.7, 654 reviews), skill badges, syllabus download CTA
- "Eligible for Master's Degree Credit" — accreditation as trust signal
- Dedicated "Program Instructors" section
- Career stats: 25% job growth projection, salary data from Talent.com
- Pricing on separate page entirely

### Fast.ai
- No pricing, no CTAs, no social proof logos
- Pure content-first: "Practical Deep Learning"
- Works for free courses with built-in audience
- Insufficient for a $499 conversion page

### Google Career Certificates
- Employer consortium logos as primary trust anchor
- 9 certificate programs in a grid
- Scholarship partner logos
- Google brand IS the trust — no instructor needed

## Recommended structure for PerformOS (derived from data)

Based on frequency analysis, the data supports this beat order:

1. Nav
2. Hero (universal: 8/8)
3. Social proof / trust signals (universal: 7/8)
4. Problem / Solution framing (derived from contrast pattern seen in AiSDR + others)
5. Learning styles / format options (PerformOS differentiator)
6. How it works (split: 4/8 — important for unfamiliar value prop)
7. Instructor credentials (majority: 6/8 — THE trust anchor for independent providers)
8. Curriculum overview (universal: 7/8)
9. Certificate / tangible outcome (differentiator — only Dataquest shows this clearly)
10. Target audience / spectrum
11. Pricing (rare: 1/8 — transparency IS the differentiator)
12. Mandatory disclosures (legal requirement — not from competitor data)
13. Comparison table (PerformOS differentiator)
14. FAQ (split: 3/8 — de-risking a $499 purchase)
15. Footer with legal notices
