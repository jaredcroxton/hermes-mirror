# Option B — Legal Rules and Compliance Boundaries

## Legal anchor (from Atticus_Counsel, 27 May 2026)

Three legal frameworks govern what PerformOS can and cannot say:

### 1. Microsoft Trademark and Brand Guidelines
**Source:** microsoft.com/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks

**Permitted:**
- Truthfully refer to Microsoft and its products and services
- Note if a product is interoperable or compatible with Microsoft products
- Include a trademark footnote: "Microsoft, Azure, and other marks are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies"
- Use Microsoft marks less prominently than your own brand

**Prohibited:**
- Don't imply affiliation, endorsement, sponsorship, or approval with or by Microsoft (THIS IS THE CRITICAL ONE)
- Don't use Microsoft's brand assets in the name of your business, product, service, app, or domain
- Don't use Microsoft's logos, icons, or designs without express written licence
- Don't use Microsoft's brand assets more prominently than your own

### 2. Australian Consumer Law
**Statute:** Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) Sch 2

- **s 18(1):** Prohibits misleading or deceptive conduct — strict liability, intent doesn't matter
- **s 29(1)(g):** Prohibits false representations about sponsorship, approval, or affiliation
- **s 219:** ACCC can demand substantiation for any claim
- **s 224:** Penalties up to $50M or 30% of adjusted turnover

**Key precedent:** ACCC v Samsung Electronics Australia Pty Ltd (2022, $14M penalty). The court applied the "overall impression" test — what a reasonable consumer would believe, not what the fine print says.

### 3. Trade Marks Act 1995 (Cth)
- **s 122:** Nominative fair use defence — permits truthful use of trademarks to describe characteristics of goods or services
- This is a DEFENCE, not a safe harbour — it doesn't prevent a claim being brought

## Option B rules (chosen by Jared)

**Option:** Instructor-credential framing. Credentials appear on the course page as instructor qualifications. The certificate is a clean PerformOS document. No third-party marks on any issued credential.

## What is SAFE

- "Your instructor, Jared Croxton, holds current Microsoft Azure AI certifications (AI-900: Azure AI Fundamentals and AI-102: Azure AI Engineer Associate)"
- "Curriculum informed by instructor's Microsoft-certified knowledge"
- "Instructor holds Microsoft AI certifications"
- "Learn from a certified AI practitioner"
- "PerformOS Certificate of Completion included"

## What is PROHIBITED (HARD STOPS)

### Endorsement claims
- "Microsoft-certified course"
- "Microsoft-approved training"
- "Official Microsoft AI course"
- "OpenAI-accredited program"
- "ChatGPT certification course"
- "Microsoft training partner"
- "In partnership with Microsoft"
- "Endorsed by Microsoft / OpenAI"
- "Powered by Microsoft"

### Credential outcome claims
- "Earn a Microsoft certification"
- "Become Microsoft certified"
- "Get Azure certified"
- "This course prepares you for the AI-900 exam"
- Placing ANY Microsoft, Azure, OpenAI, or ChatGPT certification name on the PerformOS Certificate of Completion

### Visual and logo claims
- Microsoft, Azure, OpenAI, or ChatGPT logo — in any medium, at any size
- Third-party certification badges or icons on the sales page or certificate
- Microsoft / Azure colour palette or trade dress on the certificate

### Prominence claims
- Using "Microsoft" or "Azure" more prominently than "PerformOS" on any page
- "Better than a Microsoft course"
- "The Microsoft way of learning AI"

## Mandatory requirements on the page

1. **Inline disclaimer** adjacent to any certification mention (in the instructor section)
2. **Negative disclosure block** on the sales page (below pricing): "This course is not a Microsoft, OpenAI, or ChatGPT certification program. Completion does not confer any Microsoft, OpenAI, or third-party credential. PerformOS is an independent training provider and is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or partnered with Microsoft Corporation, OpenAI, or any certification-issuing body."
3. **Trademark attribution** in the footer
4. **No certification names** on the Certificate of Completion
5. **PerformOS brand** more prominent than any third-party mark on every asset
