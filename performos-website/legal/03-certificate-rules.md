# Certificate of Completion — Design Rules and Wording

## Hard stop (never do this)

The PerformOS Certificate of Completion must NEVER contain:
- Microsoft logo
- Azure logo
- OpenAI logo
- ChatGPT logo
- Any third-party certification name (AI-900, AI-102, etc.)
- "Accredited by" or "endorsed by" language
- Reference to any external certification body

## Approved wording

```
PerformOS

Certificate of Completion

This certifies that

[Student Name]

has successfully completed the

PerformOS 12-Week Personalised AI Course

a professional development program covering practical
artificial intelligence skills, AI tooling, and applied AI concepts.

Date of completion: [DD Month YYYY]

Instructor: Jared Croxton

Issued by: PerformOS
```

## Design rules

1. PerformOS branding only — PerformOS logo, PerformOS colour palette, PerformOS typography
2. No third-party logos, icons, or marks
3. No third-party certification names anywhere on the document
4. No "accredited by" or "endorsed by" line
5. Include a certificate serial number for verifiability
6. File format: PDF, non-editable

## What the certificate IS

- A record of completion of a PerformOS professional development program
- Evidence of 12 weeks of instructor-led AI training

## What the certificate IS NOT

- A Microsoft, Azure, OpenAI, or ChatGPT certification
- A nationally recognised qualification under the Australian Qualifications Framework
- An ASQA-accredited or VET-registered credential

## On-page disclaimer (placed near certificate mockup on course page)

> "This certificate is issued by PerformOS and is not a Microsoft, OpenAI, or third-party certification."
